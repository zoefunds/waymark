# Submission

Selected primitive: semantic capability routing with definition-pinned finality.

The mechanism is reusable by agent registries, workflow contracts, and autonomous service coordinators. Its novel boundary is not “AI decides yes/no”; it creates a canonical, reusable route object whose semantic catalog is version-pinned while all consequences remain deterministic.

Research found substantial collision in fact-checking, prediction, dispute, escrow, and evidence-verification projects. Waymark is intentionally narrower and materially different: it selects among registered capabilities and exposes a composable finalized route, not a claim verdict or dispute outcome.
